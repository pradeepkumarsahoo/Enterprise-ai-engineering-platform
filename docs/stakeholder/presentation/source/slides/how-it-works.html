How it works

From scattered signals to an approved, recorded decision

1 · Sources

Read-only adapters

Logs, metrics, deploys, Jira, Git, cost exports, scanner reports

2 · Evidence

Correlate and reason

Link records, retrieve runbooks, propose hypotheses with confidence

3 · Policy

Human approval

Role checks, environment rules, approve or reject with a reason

4 · Outcome

Recorded result

Ticket, report or simulated action, written to the audit trail

Every step carries a correlation ID, so any recommendation can be traced back to the records that produced it.

Engineering Command Centre · 7

Keep this simple. Data comes in read-only. The platform links the evidence and uses AI to propose explanations, always with the source records attached. Anything consequential stops at the policy step, where a person with the right role approves or rejects with a reason. The result is recorded. Deterministic code, not the model, handles permissions, money calculations and state changes.

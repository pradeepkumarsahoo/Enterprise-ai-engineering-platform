Proposed pilot

Eight weeks, one vertical slice, one go/no-go gate

Weeks 1–2

Foundation

  • Roles, approvals and audit
  • Read-only links to [observability tool] and [Jira]
  • Measure today’s baseline

Weeks 3–5

Incident slice

  • Investigation on [2–3 services]
  • Runbook retrieval and evidence panel
  • Approval flow for simulated actions

Weeks 6–8

Evaluate

  • Shadow mode on real incidents
  • Evaluation suite and SRE review
  • Go/no-go readout to sponsors

Shadow mode: the agent investigates alongside on-call engineers and its output is compared with theirs. It takes no actions.

Engineering Command Centre · 12 · Bracketed items to be confirmed with stakeholders

The pilot is deliberately narrow. One workflow, a handful of services, read-only data and shadow mode. That gives us real evidence of value at low risk. The go/no-go at week eight decides whether to extend to a second module, which the room can pick based on the pain-points slide.

The problem

Engineering teams make high-stakes decisions from scattered evidence, under time pressure, with no consistent record of why.

Evidence is fragmented

Logs, metrics, deployments, tickets, code, cost and scan results live in separate tools with separate owners.

Context is rebuilt by hand

Every incident, design review and budget cycle starts with people copying data between screens.

AI arrives ungoverned

Assistants are adopted team by team, with no shared approval rules, evaluation or audit trail.

Engineering Command Centre · 2

This is the one sentence I want everyone to remember. The issue is not a lack of tools. Most organisations have good observability, ticketing, scanning and cost tools. The issue is that the evidence for any single decision is spread across many of them, so senior engineers spend their time assembling context rather than deciding. At the same time, AI assistants are arriving bottom-up, without common guardrails. That combination is the gap this product fills.

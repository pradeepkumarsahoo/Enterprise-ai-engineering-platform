Positioning

What it is, and what it is not

It is

  • Decision support that shows its evidence and confidence
  • Controlled automation behind human approval
  • One governed layer over the tools teams already use
  • Measured: every AI workflow has an evaluation suite

It is not

  • An autonomous operator with production access
  • A replacement for engineering judgement
  • Another monitoring, ticketing or scanning tool
  • A source of vendor quotes or individual scores

For engineering leaders who need faster, defensible decisions: a governed AI layer that connects existing tools, shows the evidence behind each recommendation and keeps people in control of every consequential action.

Engineering Command Centre · 7

This slide prevents the two most common objections. First, “will it touch production?” No. It recommends, and any consequential action requires a named approver with a written reason. Second, “do we need another tool?” It does not replace observability, Jira or scanners. It sits above them, reads their data through adapters and connects it into one evidence trail.

Why now

Three pressures are arriving at once

AI is already in use

Teams adopt copilots and chat assistants on their own. The open question is how that use is governed.

Systems keep growing

More services, deploys and dependencies mean more evidence per decision than one engineer can hold.

Spend is scrutinised

Cloud and licence costs need estimates that can be traced and explained, not just charted.

Engineering Command Centre · 4

The timing argument. If we wait, each team will have picked its own AI tool with its own data handling, and retrofitting governance becomes much harder. Starting with a small governed pilot lets us set the pattern: evidence, approval, audit, before usage scales.
